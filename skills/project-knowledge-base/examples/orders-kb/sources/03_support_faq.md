# Orders — help centre FAQ

This page is shown to customers in the help centre. Keep it short and friendly.
(Last published: unknown — the help centre does not show a date.)

## Placing an order

**How do I place an order?**
Sign in, open Orders and choose "New order". You can add a note of about 200 characters, for example delivery
instructions. If your note is longer, we keep the beginning and drop the rest.

**Can I change my order after placing it?**
You can change the note at any time. You cannot change anything else. If you need a different order, cancel this
one and place a new one.

**Why does my order say "new"?**
"new" means we have not received your payment yet. Once your payment is confirmed the order shows "paid".

## Cancelling

**Can I cancel my order?**
Yes, as long as it has not shipped. Open the order and choose "Cancel". Once the order shows "shipped" it is on
its way and can no longer be cancelled.

**I cancelled by mistake. Can I undo it?**
No. A cancelled order stays cancelled. Please place a new order.

## Seeing other orders

**I am a manager. Which orders can I see?**
Managers see every order placed in their company. Managers can also delete orders, for example duplicates.

**I opened a link to an order and got "not found", but my colleague can open it.**
Orders are visible only to the person who placed them and to the managers of the same company. If the order is
not yours and you are not a manager, the page shows "not found".

## Signing in

**I typed my password wrong a few times and now I am locked out.**
After several wrong attempts we block sign-in for that username for 15 minutes. Wait and try again. The message
is the same whether the username or the password was wrong, so we cannot tell you which one it was.

**How long does my sign-in last?**
About an hour. After that you are asked to sign in again.

## Still stuck?

Use the "Contact support" form inside the app. Do not send your password to anyone, including support.
